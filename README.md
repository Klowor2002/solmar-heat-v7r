A 3D open-world crime game built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) **gta6-01** bounty: ten story jobs, police chases, fencing, taxi fares and a neon coastal city.

## The game

Welcome to Solmar, a rain-slick neon city that never sleeps. Work your way up from
small-time runner to the name every fence and fixer in town knows:

- **Ten story jobs** — a full campaign arc, from a first nervous pickup to the
  score that makes the whole city hold its breath.
- **Police chases** — a wanted system that escalates with your heat; shake the
  tail through alleys, tunnels and the marina, or pay for your mistakes.
- **Fencing** — hot goods are worth money only if you can move them; every fence
  has a price, a mood, and a memory.
- **Taxi fares** — honest work between dishonest work: pick up fares, beat the
  clock, keep the passengers (mostly) calm.
- **A living city** — traffic, pedestrians, day-night glow, and a coastal strip
  that looks its best at 2 a.m. in the rain.

## Run it

```sh
bun run main.ts
# or
npx tsx main.ts
```

Then open the printed local URL in a browser. Progress saves automatically.
